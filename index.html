Our Story

Baked with heart, from the start.

A small kitchen, a big love for baking, and a promise that everything we make is freshly made for you — every single morning.

How we grew Get in touch
20+years of
baking

Since the very first loaf

From a home oven to a full confectionery

Bake N Frost began with a simple idea: that the best celebrations, big or small, deserve food made with real care. What started with weekend cake orders for friends and family has grown into a full confectionery serving homes, businesses and event planners across the city.

Today we bake custom cakes, daily bread and pastries, cater events with complete menus, supply wholesale partners, and train the next generation of bakers in our own academy. The scale has changed. The standard has not — every bake is made fresh, by hand, with ingredients we'd happily serve our own families.
